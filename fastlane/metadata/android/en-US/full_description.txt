This is a modern, feature-rich companion app for TubeArchivist.

It requires a connection to a self-hosted TubeArchivist server and does not provide video content on its own.

<b>Features:</b>


* Browse your TubeArchivist library
* Material You and Material 3 Expressive interface
* Search through your archived videos
* Stream videos directly from your server
* Built-in SponsorBlock support
* Library management
* Playlist management
* Multi-select functionality
